By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

Information we collect

We collect the following categories of Personal Data, directly from you and through your use of the Service:

  • Account information. Name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form) provided when you register for an account.
  • Product and usage data. Sellers you track, products you search for or analyze, tags and folders you create, and information about how you use features of the Service. This data is necessary to provide the core functionality of the Service.
  • Payment information. Payment processing is performed by Stripe, LLC. We do not receive or store your full payment card number; we retain only your subscription status and plan.
  • Technical data. Session cookies used to authenticate you, and standard server logs (IP address, browser type) maintained for security and diagnostic purposes. We do not respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

The Chrome extension

In summary: the extension reads product information from Amazon pages you visit in order to calculate profitability on your behalf. It does not monitor your browsing activity and does not collect data from any other website.

  • On Amazon product and search pages, the extension reads the product identifier (ASIN) and listed price from the page. When the panel is open, the ASIN is transmitted to our Service to retrieve product data for the calculator.
  • The eligibility check is performed only upon your explicit request (clicking "Check"). The extension queries Amazon Seller Central using your own authenticated browser session to determine whether your seller account is permitted to sell the product, and displays the result to you. Your Seller Central credentials are never accessed, collected, or transmitted to us.
  • On websites other than Amazon, the extension performs no activity unless you click its toolbar icon, which opens the calculator panel on that tab. The extension does not read the content of non-Amazon pages.
  • Panel preferences (position, theme, open or closed state) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not sell or transfer data collected by the extension to third parties. We do not use such data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's calculator functionality, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

The Company's use and transfer of information received from the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: such data is used solely to provide and improve the extension's user-facing features, is not sold or used for advertising, and is not reviewed by humans except with your consent or as necessary for security purposes or compliance with applicable law.
